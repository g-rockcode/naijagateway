      Citizen Protection Guide

      How to Stay Safe on Nigerian Government Portals

      Many Nigerians lose money or miss deadlines because of fake links shared on WhatsApp or touts charging inflated fees for free government portals.

      • 1. Check for .gov.ng or Statutory Domains: Official Federal Government portals end in .gov.ng or .mil.ng (e.g., immigration.gov.ng, army.mil.ng, nelf.gov.ng).
      • 2. Military & Paramilitary Recruitment is 100% FREE: Nigerian Army, Navy, Air Force, Customs (NCS), Civil Defence (NSCDC), Police, and FRSC never charge application fees.
      • 3. Watch the Red Deadline Tags: Portals with a red "Closing in X Days" tag on NaijaGateway are approaching their official cutoff date. Submit early to avoid portal congestion.
      • 4. Pay Only via Remita or Official Portal Gateways: Never transfer money to a personal bank account for passport renewals, driver's licenses, or federal mortgages.
